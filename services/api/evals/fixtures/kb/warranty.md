# Warranty

Juniper & Pine Outfitters is a fictional outdoor goods store used only for ResolveAI evaluations.

## What the warranty covers

Every Juniper & Pine product has a 1-year warranty against defects in materials and workmanship, counted from the delivery date. Our hiking boots, daypacks and packable rain jackets have an extended 2-year warranty.

Steel water bottles have a lifetime warranty on the vacuum seal: if a bottle stops keeping drinks hot or cold, we replace it for as long as you own it.

## What the warranty does not cover

- Normal wear and tear, such as worn-out soles, faded colours or pilling on wool.
- Damage from accidents, misuse, or improper care (for example, machine-drying a wool blanket).
- Items bought from unauthorised resellers.
- Lost or stolen items.

## How to make a warranty claim

Contact our support team with your order number, a short description of the problem, and at least one photo showing the defect. A teammate reviews the claim within 5 business days.

If the claim is approved, we repair or replace the item. If the item is no longer made, we offer a store credit for the original purchase price. We pay shipping for approved warranty replacements.

## Proof of purchase

An order number from our store is proof of purchase. For gifts, the person who received the gift can make a claim with the gift receipt or the order number.

## Care instructions

- Wool blankets and beanies: hand wash cold, dry flat. Never tumble dry.
- Packable rain jackets: machine wash cold, then tumble dry low for 20 minutes to restore the water repellency.
- Leather work gloves: wipe clean and apply leather conditioner every few months.
- Enamel mugs: dishwasher safe, but not for use on an open flame without a handle cover.
