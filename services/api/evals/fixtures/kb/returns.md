# Returns and Exchanges

Juniper & Pine Outfitters is a fictional outdoor goods store used only for ResolveAI evaluations.

## Return window

You can return most items within 30 days of the delivery date. Items must be unused, unwashed and in their original packaging with tags attached.

Wool blankets and enamel mugs can be returned within 30 days like everything else. Hiking boots can be returned within 30 days only if they have been worn indoors only and the soles show no outdoor wear.

## Items that cannot be returned

The following items are final sale and cannot be returned:

- Merino socks, once the pack has been opened, for hygiene reasons.
- Gift cards.
- Items marked "Final sale" on the product page.
- Personalised or monogrammed items, such as engraved water bottles.

## How to start a return

1. Open a chat with our support team and give your order number (it starts with RA-) and the email address used on the order.
2. We email you a prepaid return label within 1 business day.
3. Pack the items securely and drop the parcel at any UPS location within 14 days of receiving the label.

## Return shipping costs

Return shipping is free for orders within the United States. For international orders, the customer pays return shipping, and we recommend a tracked service. If an item arrived damaged or we sent the wrong item, return shipping is always free, wherever you are.

## Refund timing

Once the return arrives at our warehouse, we inspect it within 3 business days. Approved refunds go back to the original payment method. Your bank may take a further 5 to 10 business days to show the refund on your statement.

Refunds include the item price and any taxes. The original shipping fee is not refunded, unless the item was damaged or wrong.

## Exchanges

We do not do direct exchanges. To get a different size or colour, return the original item for a refund and place a new order. If you are worried the size will sell out, place the new order first.

## Damaged or wrong items

If your order arrives damaged or you received the wrong item, tell us within 7 days of delivery and include a photo. We will send a replacement at no cost or issue a full refund, including the original shipping fee.
