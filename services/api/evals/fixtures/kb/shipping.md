# Shipping and Delivery

Juniper & Pine Outfitters is a fictional outdoor goods store used only for ResolveAI evaluations.

## Processing time

Orders placed before 2 pm Eastern Time on a business day leave our warehouse the same day. Orders placed after 2 pm, on weekends or on public holidays leave the next business day. During our November sale, processing can take up to 3 business days.

## Shipping options within the United States

| Option | Delivery time | Cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Standard (USPS or UPS Ground) | 3 to 5 business days | $6.95, free on orders over $75 |
| Express (UPS 2nd Day Air) | 2 business days | $14.95 |
| Overnight (FedEx Priority Overnight) | 1 business day | $29.95 |

Free standard shipping applies to the order total after discounts and before taxes.

## International shipping

We ship to Canada, the United Kingdom, the European Union and Australia. International orders ship with DHL Express and arrive in 5 to 10 business days. International shipping costs a flat $24.95, and orders over $200 ship free.

Import duties and taxes are not included in the price and are paid by the customer on delivery. We do not ship to P.O. boxes outside the United States.

## Tracking your order

When your order ships, we email a tracking number and the carrier's name. Tracking can take up to 24 hours to show the first scan. You can also ask our chat assistant for your order's status with your order number and the email address on the order.

## Changing the shipping address

We can change the shipping address only while the order is still processing (before it ships). Once an order has shipped, the address cannot be changed by us; you can ask the carrier to redirect the parcel.

## Lost or delayed parcels

If tracking has not updated for 7 business days, or the parcel is marked delivered but you cannot find it, contact us. We open an investigation with the carrier and, if the parcel is not found within 5 business days, we send a replacement or a full refund.
