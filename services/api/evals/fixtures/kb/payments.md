# Payments and Billing

Juniper & Pine Outfitters is a fictional outdoor goods store used only for ResolveAI evaluations.

## Accepted payment methods

We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, PayPal, Apple Pay and Google Pay. Juniper & Pine gift cards can be combined with any of these. We do not accept cash on delivery, cheques or cryptocurrency.

## When you are charged

Your card is authorised when you place the order and charged when the order ships. If an order ships in more than one parcel, you may see a separate charge for each parcel.

## Currency

All prices are in US dollars. International customers are charged in US dollars, and your bank may add a foreign transaction fee.

## Pending charges and duplicate charges

A pending authorisation for a cancelled order usually disappears within 3 to 5 business days, depending on your bank. If you see the same order charged twice after it has shipped, contact our support team with your order number so a teammate can investigate; we never ask for your full card number.

## Gift cards

Gift cards are sold in amounts of $25, $50, $100 and $200. They never expire and have no fees. Gift cards cannot be exchanged for cash, except where the law requires it. A lost gift card can be replaced only if you have the original order number.

## Price adjustments

If an item you bought drops in price within 14 days of your order, we refund the difference once per item. Price adjustments do not apply to flash sales, clearance items or prices at other stores.

## Discount codes

Only one discount code can be used per order. Discount codes cannot be applied to gift cards or to an order that has already been placed.

## Invoices

An invoice is emailed with every shipped order. Business customers who need a VAT invoice can ask our support team, giving the order number and the company's VAT number.
